About The Program

Why This Matters

From data analysis to simulation, MATLAB is used across science, engineering, business, and research worldwide. This training takes you beyond lectures — you'll write real code, build models, and leave with projects you can showcase. No prior MATLAB experience needed.

Live Coding Labs

Every session is hands-on — you write MATLAB scripts, debug errors, and see results in real time. No slide decks, no theory dumps.

Real-World Projects

Model systems, analyse data, and build simulations — practical projects you can apply in any field, from research to coursework to industry.

Expert Mentors

Learn from experienced FUTMinna students and postgrads who use MATLAB daily for research, projects, and real-world problem solving.

Certificate of Completion

Receive an official certificate to strengthen your CV, NYSC, and LinkedIn profile.
